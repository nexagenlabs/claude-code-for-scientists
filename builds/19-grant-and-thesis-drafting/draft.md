## Background and preliminary data

Temozolomide reduced U87MG viability with an IC50 of 42.3 uM. Nanaomycin A
potentiates this effect at sub-toxic concentrations. Our data demonstrate
that the combination acts through inhibition of DNA methyltransferase,
establishing a mechanism for the observed synergy.

Reproducibility failures are common in preclinical cancer biology. This
approach will translate directly to patient-derived xenografts, proving
the clinical relevance of the combination.
