## Results

Temozolomide reduced viability in U87MG cells with an IC50 of 42.3 uM
(95% CI 37.0 to 47.6 uM). At 50 uM, viability fell to 92.1 per cent of
the DMSO control. All experiments were performed in triplicate (n = 3
independent plates).

Differential expression analysis across 19,996 genes identified 12 genes
with an adjusted p-value below 0.05.
